What is a ket? It’s a vector in Hilbert Space. But what does that mean?  A vector is a column of numbers - in Hilbert Space these are complex numbers (a + ib), where {% raw %} $i^2 = -1$ {% endraw %} and a,b are real numbers. And Hilbert space is a type of vector space consisting of ket vectors or kets for short.

Here are the axioms of Hilbert space we need.

1) Closure under addition. The sum of any 2 kets is also a ket. Kets look like this{% raw %} $ |A\rangle + |B\rangle = |C\rangle $ {% endraw %}.



The {% raw %} $\langle bra|\hat{c}|ket \rangle$ {% endraw %} notation is due to Dirac and is brilliant!
